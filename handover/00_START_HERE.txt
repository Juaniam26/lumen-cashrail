TRUTH RULES
1. Source claim: preserve source URL/provider record + retrieval time + observed fact.
2. No live evidence: BLOCKED_NO_LIVE_SOURCE. Do not fabricate research.
3. Drafted or copied email != sent.
4. Verified send requires provider message id / provider acceptance evidence.
5. Manually entered money != verified cash.
6. Verified cash requires transaction / settlement evidence.
7. Refunds and direct costs stay visible; do not rewrite history.

MONEY MODEL
Net contribution = verified gross cash
- partner payouts
- payment fees
- attributable data/outbound/model costs
- refunds
- other directly attributable costs

PARTNER SALES PAYMENT PATH
Customer pays Partner through Partner's checkout/invoice.
Partner pays Cashrail the agreed commission after cleared attributable funds.
Cashrail does not receive customer settlement for a third party and forward it onward.

HOW TO ALLOCATE WORK
Do not kill a lane because a clock hit 18 hours.
Diagnose the funnel:
- delivered / no replies -> list, sender, channel, copy
- replies / no qualified -> targeting or offer
- qualified / no signed -> trust, proof, pricing, agreement
- signed / no cash -> closing, attribution, fulfillment, payment path
Move effort toward observed net contribution and credible path to verified cash.
