COMPLIANCE BOUNDARY — REAL ESTATE IS DISABLED IN CASHRAIL
Canonical status: config/revenue-runtime.json -> real_estate = disabled

This file is retained to explain the stay-out boundary. It is NOT a current revenue lane, an authorization to relabel brokerage activity, or a workaround guide.

You do not have a South Carolina real estate license.

YOU CANNOT
- Call yourself an agent, realtor, listing specialist, or co-lister
- Talk to sellers as if you can list their house
- Show houses, host opens, or walk sellers through licensed activity
- Explain or negotiate listing agreements, contracts, or commissions
- Take a cut of a commission, a referral fee, or a percentage of the listing side
- Advertise a property you do not own as though acting as the listing broker/agent
- Pay or get paid under the table when a house closes
- Pretend to be a buyer to obtain access or induce a seller conversation

SC statutory references previously used by this kit include S.C. Code §40-57-135 and §40-57-710. Current text should be checked against the official South Carolina Code before relying on a specific legal conclusion.

PERMITTED RESEARCH/ADMIN BOUNDARY
Public-record research, public listing research, internal diagnosis, and drafting/admin support may be possible when performed within applicable law and under a properly authorized business relationship. That does not reactivate real estate as a Cashrail revenue workstream.

CURRENT CASHRAIL RULE
Do not prospect, market, negotiate, list, refer for a commission, or create percentage-based real-estate compensation under Cashrail.

If the owner later becomes licensed and intentionally creates a separate compliant real-estate business, that should be a separately reviewed project with its own canonical runtime. Do not silently turn L6 back on here.
