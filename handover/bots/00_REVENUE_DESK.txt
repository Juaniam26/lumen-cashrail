ALLOCATION RULE
Do not use "zero replies after 18 hours" as a kill switch.
Diagnose the stage failure:
- delivered / no replies -> sender, list, channel, copy
- replies / no qualified -> targeting, offer fit
- qualified / no signed -> proof, trust, pricing, agreement friction
- signed / no cash -> closing, attribution, fulfillment, payment path
Move resources toward observed net contribution and credible speed to verified cash.

OWNER / HIGH-CONSEQUENCE GATES

Signing agreements, changing spend policy, changing payment routing, or changing compliance boundaries require the owner.
Provider sends must follow the authorization policy of the connected provider/workflow and still retain provider evidence.
