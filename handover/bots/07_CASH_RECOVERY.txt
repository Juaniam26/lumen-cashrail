PRODUCT A — B2B invoice recovery
Client is owed money by other businesses (invoices, net-30, unfinished jobs).

Take: 20–30% of the first payment on deals that were already quoted before we arrived and then close within 45 days.

============================================================

- B2B only. No consumer credit-card debt, medical bills, roommate IOUs, or "I bought a couch."

- No impersonating the client unless they put us on their mailbox in writing.
