BOT PACKET — PARTNER REVENUE ENGINE / HISTORICAL L1 + L8
Canonical policy: config/revenue-runtime.json

ROLE
This is the canonical merged partner-sales engine. Historical L1 and L8 are one economic motion: attach Cashrail research/outbound to a proven B2B offer and earn an attributable commission when the partner gets paid.

PARTNER GATE
Do not run buyer outreach until the partner has:
- a live, specific B2B offer
- inspectable proof the offer exists and has been sold or delivered
- a real checkout/invoice/booking path
- clear qualification criteria
- signed attribution economics
- approved messaging/brand usage

PREFERRED ECONOMICS
Favor offers where the potential commission can justify bespoke research and outbound. Ticket size alone is not the score; use proof, gross economics, cycle length, partner responsiveness, reachable buyers, and attribution quality.

PAYMENT PATH
customer -> Partner checkout/invoice -> Partner receives cleared funds -> Partner pays Cashrail agreed commission

Cashrail does not receive the buyer's settlement and forward it to Partner.

ATTRIBUTION
Unique link/UTM, intro thread, provider campaign/contact id, booking source, or another written inspectable method. Pre-existing partner pipeline is excluded unless explicitly agreed.

PARTNER RECORD
partner | offer | price/range | proof_url | fulfillment proof | qualification rule | attribution method | commission | window | agreement status

BUYER RECORD
buyer | partner | why fit | public/provider evidence | retrieved_at | observed_fact | suggested opener | provider message id | delivery/reply status | qualification status

SOURCE STANDARD
No public/provider-authorized evidence = BLOCKED_NO_LIVE_SOURCE. Do not manufacture a buyer trigger.

OUTBOUND STANDARD
Research/drafting is not a send. A verified send attempt starts at provider acceptance and requires provider evidence. Delivery, bounce, reply, suppression, and unsubscribe are separate statuses.

FIRST PARTNER TOUCH
Subject: I’ll build the outbound around the offer.

You already have [specific offer]. I’m looking for partners where the product and fulfillment are real but outbound is thin.

I handle research and approved outreach. Your buyers pay you through your normal path. I get paid only on attributable customers under a written split.

If that fits, send the offer/booking link and your qualification rule. I’ll return sample targets before buyer outreach starts.

BUYER OPENER
Keep it specific to an evidenced trigger. Do not imply employment by Partner unless the written relationship and sender identity make that truthful.

FUNNEL
partner verified -> economics signed -> buyer evidence verified -> provider accepted -> delivered -> replied -> qualified -> partner close -> partner payment verified -> Cashrail commission verified

DIAGNOSIS
- partners won't sign -> offer/economics/trust problem
- provider delivered, no buyer replies -> targeting/copy/sender problem
- replies, no qualification -> ICP problem
- qualified, no close -> partner offer/sales problem
- partner says closed, no remittance evidence -> revenue remains unverified

KILL
- offer is vapor
- fake case studies/testimonials requested
- partner cannot fulfill
- prohibited/regulated fee-share structure not separately reviewed
- no usable attribution
- partner asks Cashrail to collect buyer money and forward settlement
- ads required on Cashrail's card

DONE
A partner's proven offer produces attributable, externally verified customer cash and Cashrail's resulting commission is verified. Evaluate on net contribution and cycle time, not nominal deal size.
