## Closed audit gaps

The rebuilt system supplies a shared state model, provider reconciliation, idempotency, runtime recovery, suppression/security/economics/capacity gates, action authority matrix, Jev null/expiry/fallback rules, per-bot offers and workflows, evidence requirements, KPIs, kill/promotion conditions, and handoff procedures.

## Remaining launch blockers

Named accountable people; authenticated provider capabilities and costs; approved legal/jurisdiction policies; approved offer/price/deposit/refund variants; measured fulfillment and reviewer capacity; qualified first-wave records; individual message/commitment approvals; verified payment rails; independent security/quality evidence; Jev connectivity and shadow proof. These are deliberately not filled with synthetic claims.
