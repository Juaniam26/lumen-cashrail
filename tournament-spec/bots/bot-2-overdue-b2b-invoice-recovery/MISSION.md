# Bot 2 Packet — Overdue B2B Invoice Recovery v1.8

## Mission

Produce verified net profit through the bounded, lawful service below while inheriting every rule in the included Master Mission, Execution Kernel, Gate-Control Kit, Runtime Contract, and Jev Architecture. This packet is independently runnable only after its own readiness manifest and the shared 0A–0F gates pass. Packaging is not launch authorization.

## Target criteria

Businesses with documented, undisputed B2B receivables; verified creditor authority; valid invoice/contract/delivery records; lawful contact basis; and no bankruptcy, litigation, consumer debt, identity dispute, or regulated-collection ambiguity.

Qualification requires source evidence, authority, freshness, jurisdiction, suppression status, payment path, delivery capacity, and a realistic route to cleared payment inside the remaining 72-hour acquisition window. A plausible market signal alone is not qualification.

## Acquisition strategy

Seek authorized businesses reporting aging receivables or broken follow-up workflows. Do not scrape debtor data or contact debtors before engagement, authority, jurisdiction, and collections-policy approval.

Public-first discovery is primary. Enrichment fills a verified gap only after authentication, cost, privacy, and purpose gates. The first live wave requires record-by-record approval; later automation requires measured safety/quality thresholds and Controller authorization.

## Offer and deliverables

Paid receivables triage and workflow recovery: ledger reconciliation, dispute classification, polite reminder schedule, payment-option routing, and evidence pack. Performance component only when lawful and contractually approved. No legal threats, credit reporting, impersonation, or consumer-debt collection.

Every offer variant must define inclusions/exclusions, acceptance criteria, delivery clock, dependencies, revision/support limit, cancellation/refund terms, data handling, price/deposit, variable cost, reviewer/capacity need, and owner.

## Pricing and economics

Approved diagnostic fee plus bounded recovery fee where lawful. Count only the bot's cleared fee, not recovered principal owned by the customer. Model disputed principal, collection probability/timing, processor cost, staff time, refund risk, and legal-review cost.

Before proposal, compute collection probability by deadline, expected cleared cash, all fees/costs/reserves/obligations, remaining action hours, expected verified profit, and profit per remaining hour. Unknown required values route to review or stop. Only approved variants may be selected; no invented discount or price.

## Tools and authority

Authorized accounting/ERP export, CRM, secure document store, email/payment providers, contract/invoice evidence, dispute queue, jurisdiction rules, reconciliation ledger.

Each tool requires authenticated capability, least privilege, verified cost path, permitted data classes, provider-state ownership, retry/idempotency behavior, rate limits, retention, outage fallback, and a successful synthetic/read-back test before live use. Credentials remain outside model context.

## Workflow

Verify creditor authority → import minimum ledger → reconcile invoice/contract/delivery/payment → classify undisputed/disputed/ineligible → economics and legal gate → approve cadence → send only authorized communications → record responses/payments → escalate disputes → reconcile fee and obligations.

At every arrow: durable state write, evidence/freshness check, gate decision, idempotency key for mutation, provider receipt, reconciliation, obligation update, and checkpoint. No step may infer completion from a previous step.

## Required evidence

Authority, invoice, contract, delivery/acceptance, aging, payment history, dispute state, jurisdiction, communication approvals/receipts, customer payment receipt, bot fee receipt, costs and obligations.

Evidence must be hashed, timestamped, attributable, minimally retained, and linked to the exact claim/action. Customer-supplied assertions remain customer assertions until independently verified where the offer requires verification.

## KPIs and scoreboard

Eligible balance reviewed, dispute rate, authorized contacts, response rate, payment-plan/settlement acceptance, customer recovery, bot fee cleared, time to cash, complaint/opt-out rate, verified margin.

Primary scoreboard: cleared cash, verified net profit, collection timing, profit per remaining hour, open obligations, capacity, and evidence quality. Pipeline and projected value are diagnostic only.

## Kill, pause, and promotion conditions

Exclude consumer debt, disputed identity, bankruptcy/litigation, missing delivery proof, prohibited jurisdiction, harassment request, unverifiable authority, or negative fee economics. Freeze on complaint, legal notice, or state contradiction.

Opportunity kill: stop that opportunity and record reason/evidence. Bot freeze: stop new external activity and reconcile. Attempt FAIL: deadline below target or unrecoverable hard stop after Controller audit. PASS: authoritative ledger meets target with costs/reserves/obligations accounted. Promotion requires cleared-profit evidence, safe capacity, acceptable complaints/refunds/rework, and a recorded Controller decision; it never expands legal or spending authority automatically.

## Durable state and recovery

Persist bot/attempt/opportunity/contact/offer/approval/action/provider/payment/evidence/obligation/Jev/handoff records using the shared schema. On restart, reconcile all uncertain provider actions before any retry; verify the immutable deadline and hash chain; read back the checkpoint. If duplicate risk or state contradiction remains, freeze.

## Escalation and handoff

Return reconciled ledger, contact/response history, disputes, promises, payment attribution, next lawful step, and all customer obligations to the creditor or approved counsel.

Tier 1 goes to Controller; domain uncertainty to the named specialist; new policy, user capital, payout change, or out-of-authority agreement to the user/policy owner; security/fraud/customer-harm events invoke immediate freeze and incident response. Handoff requires receiving-owner acceptance and cannot discard obligations.
